ONE LIFE

One character. One life. Make it count.

A dark fantasy MMO where death is final. Fight, trade and build your name in a world that remembers, because when you fall, you stay fallen.

The one rule

You only get one.

Most games let you respawn and shrug it off. ONE LIFE doesn't. Every fight is a choice, every trip past the walls is a risk, and every character has a story that ends exactly once.

I

Death is final

No respawns, no revives for sale. When your character dies, their story is over, and the world remembers how.

II

What you carry, you lose

Gold and gear on your back fall with you. Bank what matters and keep your vault; gamble the rest on the road.

III

Every choice counts

Your class, your path and your gear are yours for life. There's no reset button, so make it legendary.

Choose your fighter

Three classes. Twelve paths.

Every class branches into four paths you master as you rise. Choose well. It's for life.

Steel · Shield · Nerve

Warrior

Stand in the front line, hold the gate and cleave through anything that gets close. Built to take a hit and keep swinging.

Bow · Blades · Speed

Ranger

Strike from the treeline, mark your prey and vanish before it turns. The wilds are your hunting ground.

Ember · Ward · Wrath

Mage

Command fire, shield your allies and turn the tide of a fight with a single spell. Fragile, but devastating.

The world

Beyond the walls, the dark is waiting.

You wake on the burnt edge of the Ash with a borrowed blade. Emberhold's lanterns are the last light before the wilds: haunted groves, flooded tunnels, a buried foundry, and a war-torn march where the dead don't stay quiet.

The people of Emberhold
What awaits

An MMO built on stakes.

Group dungeons

The Silt Warren, the Sunken Foundry, the Grain Cellar: sprawling mini-raids built for a crew, with bosses that fight dirty.

The Cinder March

A PvP warzone. When a player falls there, their gear hits the ground for anyone brave enough to grab it.

Legendary loot

Legendary armour you can see on your character, and super-rare weapons with tricks of their own. Some drop once in a very long while.

A living world

Day and night, storms rolling in, world bosses on the hunt, siege nights at the gate, and events that change the map.

Craft & trade

Forge, brew, cook and build. Sell to real players in a market they run, and take on work for hire.

A home of your own

Claim a plot, build it up piece by piece, and keep your treasures safe in the vault. It's the one thing death can't take.

Web3, done right

Real gold. Real stakes.

ONE LIFE runs on Solana. Your wallet is your account, and the plan is for the gold you earn by playing to be the ONE LIFE token, one for one.

01

Sign in with your wallet

Connect Phantom and you're in. No email, no password. Your character follows your wallet to any device.

02

Enter with SOL

Each life costs $20, paid in SOL straight from your wallet. No middleman, no card, settled on-chain.

03

Earn real gold

Planned: 1 gold in the game = 1 ONE LIFE token. Quest, fight, craft and trade for it. Die carrying it, and it's gone.

04

Move it in and out Planned

Deposit tokens to fund your adventures, or withdraw the gold you've banked back to your wallet.

Every life fuels the token

Entry fees, put back into the token.

Using entry fees to buy back the ONE LIFE token is a planned feature. The more lives lived in the world, the more the game can put back into it. No amounts are set yet.
