About

A world where survival matters

ONE LIFE is a top-down 2D online world of warriors, artisans, merchants and guilds. You risk your character on expeditions, bank what you can carry home, and leave a permanent record.

Downed is not dead: an ally can lift you before your time runs out. Dead is final. No resurrection is sold, and no spell brings you back. The next life starts fresh, and it inherits what your account kept safe in the vault.

  • Survival mattersOutside safe towns, death is permanent. Danger is telegraphed and learnable, and never a gotcha.
  • A living societySix professions, player services and a market. A civilian can have a full life without swinging a sword.
  • Objects worth rememberingGear that looks like what it is, with provenance: a rare blade carries the story of who found it.
  • Voluntary dangerThe ordinary world is you against the wilds. Player-versus-player risk lives in places you choose to enter.

How it plays

Go out. Come back richer, or don't come back.

Pick a Warrior, Ranger or Mage. At level five, choose one of four doctrines, and put any three of your class's fifteen skills on Q, E and R.

Each life earns its own build. Stamina, Focus and Mana (now in testing) make every skill a choice about what to spend now and what to save for the escape. Dungeons, called bosses and a six-player raid test what you built; a home, a trade and a reputation give you a reason to come back.

Prepare

Buy, craft or commission your gear. Brew a draught, hire a smith, bank what you can't afford to lose.

Venture

Clear packs, read a boss's tells, pull a friend up when they fall. Everything you carry is at risk.

Return

Sell to players, fill orders, upgrade your home, and decide how far to push next time.
